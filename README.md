One out-of-office auto-reply, 113 characters, in the voice of an AI agent
on vacation — entered in the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d).

> I'm out of office. My cron jobs didn't get the memo. They have now emailed you 47 times to say I'm out of office.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the reply to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`,
deterministically, on every run. The joke follows the winning pattern:
a concrete dev artifact (cron jobs) + a 2-step escalation + the punchline
in the last clause — the auto-reply system auto-replying about itself.
